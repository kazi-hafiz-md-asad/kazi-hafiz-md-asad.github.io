Research statement

Efficiency claims should survive careful scrutiny.

I investigate how much model capacity is genuinely necessary and how that claim can be demonstrated through rigorous experimentation. My work combines resource-aware model development with transparent analysis of quality, memory, runtime, stability, and generalization.

I am especially interested in methods that make strong models practical without hiding trade-offs: knowledge distillation, low-rank adaptation, quantization, structured comparison, and higher-order representations.
